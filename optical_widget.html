Optico-Mechanical Rays
ML wavefronts + Killing flow + Busemann target

What you are seeing — ambient lift

Rays are characteristics of the hyperbolic flow \(\dot z = K_t(z) - \nabla_{\mathrm{hyp}}U_t(z)\), with \(U_t=-(\varepsilon(t)/2)\log(\varphi_t/\psi_t)\). The Busemann factor \(\varphi_0\) anchors \(t=0\), while \(\psi_t\approx \exp(-d_g(z,z_0)^2/(4\Sigma_{\mathrm{rem}}(t)))\) sharpens as \(\Sigma_{\mathrm{rem}}(t)=\int_t^{\infty}\varepsilon(u)\,du\) shrinks. Through the log-ratio this yields a time-weighted repulsion of size \(\varepsilon/\Sigma_{\mathrm{rem}}\). The result is then lifted to the ambient plane by \(\;x=\frac{2\,\operatorname{artanh}(|z|)}{|z|}z\). Dashed curves are ML wavefronts: rays are reparameterized by hyperbolic arclength, matched at common \(\tau\), and averaged across perturbations.

What you are seeing — Poincaré disk

The same dynamics drawn in the disk itself. Early times are dominated by the Busemann boundary factor \(\varphi_0\), mid-time by the solenoidal Killing field \(K_t\), and late times by the bridge-to-\(z_0\) factor \(\psi_t\) as \(\Sigma_{\mathrm{rem}}\) contracts. Dotted circles are horocycles (Busemann level sets), and the dashed radius shows the direction to \(\xi\).
