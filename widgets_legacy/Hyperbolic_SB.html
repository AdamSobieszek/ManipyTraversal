About

This widget is a qualitative visualization of a hyperbolic Schrödinger bridge / entropic transport on the Poincaré ball \( \mathbb B^d \), displayed as a 2D slice, with target “direction at infinity” \( \zeta\in\partial\mathbb B^d \).

Hyperbolic (Fisher) metric: \( g_{ij}(z)=\tfrac{4}{(1-\|z\|^2)^2}\delta_{ij} \), \( g^{ij}(z)=\tfrac{(1-\|z\|^2)^2}{4}\delta^{ij} \), and \( \rho_{\mathbb H}(r)=2\,\operatorname{atanh}(r) \).

Variational principle: \( \mathcal S[z]=\int_0^T\big(\tfrac12\langle\dot z,\dot z\rangle_{g(z)}-\Phi(t,z)\big)\,dt \), so \( \nabla_{\dot z}\dot z=-\operatorname{grad}_g\Phi \). For endpoints at infinity one uses Busemann / Poisson-kernel potentials: \( b_\zeta(z)=\log\!\big(\tfrac{1-\|z\|^2}{\|z-\zeta\|^2}\big) \), \( \Phi=-\alpha\,b_\zeta \).
