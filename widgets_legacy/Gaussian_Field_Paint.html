How it works

The vector field is stored on a grid of sample points inside the disk. Each drawn stroke is split into segments; each segment contributes a vector in its direction, weighted by a Gaussian kernel \( \exp(-\|x-x_0\|^2/(2\sigma^2)) \) centered at the segment midpoint.

Arrows are colored by strength, and their length scales like \( \sqrt{\|v\|} \).
