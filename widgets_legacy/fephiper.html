What you are seeing

The drift is a time-warped hyperbolic natural-gradient ascent of the Poisson kernel toward the boundary point \( \zeta \), plus a local saddle correction that cancels the longitudinal drift at \( w_s \) (so \( w_s \) is a true saddle fixed point).

Noise is injected in the (Euclidean) tangent approximation and scaled by your SNR knob. Lower SNR → easier transverse escape; higher SNR → trajectories stick to the stable manifold and pass through.
