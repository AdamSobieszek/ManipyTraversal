Optico-Mechanical Rays
action wavefronts + Killing flow + Busemann target

What you are seeing — ambient lift

Rays are characteristics of the hyperbolic flow \(\dot z = K_t(z) - \nabla_{\mathrm{hyp}}\Phi_t(z)\), evolved directly on the Poincaré disk and then lifted to the ambient plane by \(\;x=\frac{2\,\operatorname{artanh}(|z|)}{|z|}z\). Dashed curves are equal-action wavefronts \(S=\mathrm{const}\) with \(S(t)=\int_0^t(\tfrac12|\dot z-K_t|_g^2-\Phi_t)\,dt\).

What you are seeing — Poincaré disk

The same dynamics drawn in the disk itself. Early times are dominated by the Gaussian potential \(\Phi_0\), mid-time by the solenoidal Killing field \(K_t\), and late times by the Busemann pull \(\Phi_\infty\). Dotted circles are horocycles (Busemann level sets) tangent at the ideal point \(\xi\).

Model equations

\[ \dot z = K_t(z) - \nabla_{\mathrm{hyp}}\Phi_t(z), \qquad \nabla_{\mathrm{hyp}}\Phi=\frac{(1-|z|^2)^2}{4}\nabla_E\Phi \]
\[ S(t)=\int_0^t\left(\tfrac12|\dot z-K_t|_g^2-\Phi_t(z)\right)\,dt \]
\[ \Phi_t=(1-\eta)\Phi_0+\eta\Phi_\infty,\qquad \eta(t)=\tfrac12(1+\tanh(\alpha t)) \]
\[ \Phi_0=\tfrac{\beta}{2}d_g(z,0)^2, \quad d_g(z,0)=2\,\operatorname{artanh}(|z|), \quad \Phi_\infty=-\kappa\,b_\xi(z) \]
\[ K_{A,Z}(z)=Az-\langle Z,z\rangle z+\tfrac12(1+|z|^2)Z,\quad A^\top=-A \]
