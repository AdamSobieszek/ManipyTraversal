Model (2D visualization, \(d\)-dependent bias)

We simulate in the Poincaré disk with \(\zeta(t)\in SO(2)\) (angle \(\phi\)). Order parameter uses the thermodynamic-limit LMS formula \(Z(z)=K\,f_d(|z|)\,z\) where \(f_d\) is a Gauss hypergeometric ratio. For \(d=2\), \(f_d\equiv 1\) (the \((d-1)\) constant turns into \(1\)).

\(\zeta\) evolves by “Thomas/Wigner precession”: \(\dot\phi=\omega-\mathrm{cross}(\zeta w,\,Z)\). Noise is injected in a tangent (Euclidean) approximation with \(\sigma\approx \|\text{drift}\|/\mathrm{SNR}\).
